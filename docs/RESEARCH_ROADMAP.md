# From open questions to reproducible research

FrontierAtlas prioritizes testable intermediate results before ambitious claims of solutions. The following are proposals, not established discoveries.

| Domain | Entry | Starting research target | Falsification / validation gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physics | P02 | Conditional regularity estimates under a defined vorticity geometry | Complete estimates under explicit assumptions; known counterexample checks |
| Physics | P17 | Energy-stable and transferable turbulence closures | Out-of-distribution geometries and physically required conservation tests |
| Chemistry | C04 | Structural identifiability in partially observed reaction networks | Independent species measurements break otherwise provable parameter degeneracy |
| Chemistry | C10 | Interphase reaction mechanism discrimination | Competing mechanistic models make prospectively distinguishable measurements |
| Genetics | G06 | Recover higher-order epistasis from limited interventions | Held-out genotype combinations outperform additive and pairwise controls |
| Genetics | G09 | Generalize perturbation response models | Explicitly novel combinatorial perturbations with calibration checks |
| Psychology | PS19 | Identify causal effects in longitudinal observational systems | Bias bounds or experimental interventions exclude observationally equivalent models |
| Psychology | PS17 | Predict replication robustness | Prespecified external replication cohorts and prospective replication evaluation |
| Physiology | PH05 | Separate latent causes of insulin resistance | Identifiability proof plus independently measured hormone and substrate fluxes |
| Physiology | PH20 | Measure physiological reserve under controlled stress | Prospective validation across instruments, ages and confounders |

## A six-phase research protocol

1. **Literature and status audit:** identify original formulation, strongest known partial results, counterexamples and recent claims.
2. **Formalization:** specify variables, units, model, boundary conditions, measurements, interventions and assumptions.
3. **Obstruction analysis:** establish what is not identifiable, estimable or provable under current information.
4. **Restricted theorem or prediction:** choose a tractable class with an explicit validation or falsification test.
5. **Reproducibility:** create synthetic baselines, tests, uncertainty estimates and public experiment specifications.
6. **External scrutiny:** seek independent peer review, replication or disproof; update the atlas accordingly.

## Reusable template

For a proposed contribution, document: **research question; strongest known result; assumptions; theorem/hypothesis; proposed method; counterexample checks; expected observable; null/alternative models; data provenance; risk and ethical review; criteria for rejection; references.**
