## Problems

### P01 · 4D Yang–Mills existence and mass gap

**Domain:** Physics · **Topic:** Mathematical physics · **Subtopic:** Rigorous field & fluid theory  
**Classification:** Formally open · **Research approach:** Theory / proof

**Open question:** Can an interacting four-dimensional continuum Yang–Mills quantum field theory be constructed rigorously with a positive spectral mass gap?

**Scientific rationale:** The Standard Model uses non-Abelian gauge theory, but the full constructive proof is missing.

**Bottleneck:** Nonperturbative continuum limits, gauge invariance and uniform spectral estimates resist existing tools.

**Research direction:** Study rigorously controlled lattice limits; isolate conditions preserving reflection positivity and a volume-independent gap.

**First decisive test:** Prove a uniform bound on spectral gaps for an explicitly restricted class before attempting 4D continuum control.

**Sources:**
- [R1] [Yang–Mills and the Mass Gap — Clay Mathematics Institute](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/)

### P02 · Unforced 3D Navier–Stokes regularity

**Domain:** Physics · **Topic:** Mathematical physics · **Subtopic:** Rigorous field & fluid theory  
**Classification:** Formally open · **Research approach:** Theory / proof

**Open question:** Do all smooth divergence-free finite-energy initial data yield global smooth unforced solutions, or can singularities form?

**Scientific rationale:** Resolving this would clarify a central issue in PDE theory and idealized fluid mechanics.

**Bottleneck:** Vortex stretching and supercritical estimates prevent known energy controls from bounding all gradients.

**Research direction:** Seek scale-critical a priori estimates or exclude concentrated vorticity geometry; verify all claimed inequalities.

**First decisive test:** Derive a new coercive inequality under a clearly stated symmetry or alignment condition.

### P03 · Quantum gravity with observable predictions

**Domain:** Physics · **Topic:** Mathematical physics · **Subtopic:** Quantum spacetime & horizons  
**Classification:** Foundational open question · **Research approach:** Model + experimental validation

**Open question:** Which mathematically consistent framework reproduces general relativity and quantum field theory in their domains and predicts a discriminating effect?

**Scientific rationale:** General relativity and quantum theory are independently predictive yet conceptually incomplete in combination.

**Bottleneck:** No accessible high-energy experimental regime and no unique model-selection principle.

**First decisive test:** Specify an observable where two candidate frameworks give non-overlapping uncertainty intervals.
